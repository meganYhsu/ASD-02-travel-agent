# Travel Preparation Knowledge

## Document checks

Check passport expiry dates well before departure and verify destination-specific validity rules with the destination government's official immigration website. Carry only the travel documents needed for the trip and keep secure backup copies separately from the originals.

## Packing

Build a packing list from the destination, trip duration, expected climate, planned activities, accommodation, and transport. Keep essential medication and critical documents in carry-on luggage. Check airline restrictions before packing batteries, liquids, medication, or specialist equipment.

## Pre-trip tasks

Before departure, confirm bookings, insurance coverage, emergency contacts, payment access, roaming or connectivity, and transport to the airport or station. Review unfinished tasks several days before departure so there is time to resolve problems.

## Entry requirements

Entry, visa, vaccination, and passport-validity requirements can change. Information in this project is demonstration data and is not official immigration advice. Travellers must verify requirements using official government, embassy, immigration, and public-health sources.
